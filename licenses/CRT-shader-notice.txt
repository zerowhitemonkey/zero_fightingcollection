The CRT filter (scanlines + blur) in src/gfxb_d3d11.c and src/gfxb_gl.c is a simplified port of
"CRT-ZERO", itself a simplified version of CRT-Frutbunn, which is adapted from frutbunn's
"Another CRT shader" on Shadertoy: https://www.shadertoy.com/view/XdyGzR

Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/).

for derivatives.
